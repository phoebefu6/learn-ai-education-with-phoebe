Learn AI Education with Phoebe

Two tracks into teaching that survives AI

Sixty percent of teachers already use AI - and the same tool that doubles learning in one classroom quietly erases it in the next. The difference is design, and design is teachable. A leader track gives deans, principals, and L&D heads the evidence, the policy patterns, and the governance rails - no detector required. An educator track takes teachers, instructional designers, and L&D practitioners through one real redesign: Riverview's Grade 9 persuasive-essay unit, rebuilt end to end with the four levers - policy, AI roles, redesigned assessment, privacy rails. A live classroom simulator runs on the key pages: watch 12 classroom moments fail and recover as you toggle the levers - and discover for yourself why the fifth toggle, the AI detector, never helps.
